## Detection and observation
Alert or investigate spikes by route/client/version, repeated low-frequency property probes, and divergence between gateway and service decisions. Validation: Send a matrix of valid, malformed, boundary, unknown, and read-only synthetic fields; assert decisions, error stability, and no mutation.
