## Learning outcome

Model an authentication protocol as actors, messages, state transitions, verifier binding, expected outcomes, and evidence limits.

## Scope statement

Includes protocol ceremony mechanics, RP/verifier binding, authentication-versus-authorization boundaries, and WebAuthn/OAuth identity distinctions. Excludes deployment-specific effectiveness and live protocol testing.

## Prerequisites

- `CC-D01-001`

## Internals and mechanisms

Authentication is treated as a stateful trust-boundary process: actors exchange bounded messages or challenges, the verifier validates proof under a scoped identity context, and success establishes an authentication result without automatically granting authorization.
