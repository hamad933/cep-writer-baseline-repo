## 5. Reusable foundation architecture — canonical interpretation
The Big Boss 40 shared cores are retained as **conceptual ownership contracts**, not as mandatory classes/services or historical TypeScript files. Each must be implemented with the lightest executable mechanism that satisfies its contract. The all-surface matrix contains all 23 surfaces so omission cannot masquerade as N/A.

Three tiers are mandatory:
1. **Global shared foundation** — shell, regions/panes, preferences, locale/direction/Bidi, semantic commands/actions, transients/focus/a11y, status/epistemic state, discovery, production-boundary rules.
2. **Surface-family engines** — StructuredContent/Editor, Spatial, Analytical, Operational, Review/Decision, Audit/Provenance, Transaction History, Clipboard/Trust, Notes/Annotation where admitted.
3. **Thin domain adapters** — W01–W05 surface semantics, canonical objects, domain states, API/persistence/provider boundaries. Generic presentation mechanics never own domain truth.

## 7. What future Work must NOT do
- Do not start from a latest-looking Blueprint or candidate.
- Do not treat W03 v3.4, Visualize candidates, or W01/W02 Production candidates as final design authority.
- Do not reintroduce Arabic-first, fixed RTL, fixed theme, fixed toolbar placement, or similar defaults as immutable laws.
- Do not carry Big Boss historical framework file paths into the new foundation by assumption.
- Do not create a second state/command/event owner for a generic mechanic.
- Do not use `FILE_EXISTS`, `handler exists`, `mapping PASS`, or `Writer PASS` as interaction/product acceptance.
- Do not generalize Library's Knowledge domain semantics into unrelated domains; inherit mechanics, not business truth.
- Do not expose internal donor/core/debug labels as normal permanent product chrome.

## 8. Remaining intentionally open items
This canonicalization deliberately does **not** freeze the final technology stack, DB, physical directories/classes, or API framework. Those should be resolved in the representative Foundation Proof-of-Fit. It also does not declare non-Library Blueprints accepted. Their outstanding Owner decisions/requests must feed the relevant family/domain adapters.
