# STOP / ESCALATION RULES

Do not stop merely because one source is large or a route/browser provider is unavailable. Use available file/code rendering evidence and classify proof correctly.

Stop a specific mutation/decision and record `OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED` only when:
- two current Owner-level requirements conflict;
- domain canonical owner is genuinely unresolved;
- a proposed shared abstraction would erase domain truth;
- a technology introduces an irreversible dependency without measured need;
- accepted donor identity/hash mismatches;
- a required provider/native capability cannot be truthfully simulated or bounded.

Continue all other safe work rather than stopping the entire mission.
